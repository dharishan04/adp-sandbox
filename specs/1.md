# Add reading history for a sensor over a time range

## Requirements
- **FR-001** `GET /sensors/{id}/history?from&to` (`from`, `to` ISO-8601 timestamps) answers 200 with `{ "sensorId": string, "readings": [{ "at": date-time, "value": number }] }`, containing only readings of that sensor within the range, oldest first.
- **FR-002** Both bounds are inclusive: a reading whose `at` equals `from` or `to` is included.
- **FR-003** An unknown sensor answers 404 `{ "error": "unknown_sensor" }`.
- **FR-004** A range longer than 7 days (`to - from > 7 days`) answers 400 `{ "error": "range_too_large" }`; a range of exactly 7 days is accepted.
- **FR-005** A range within the maximum that contains no readings answers 200 `{ "sensorId": string, "readings": [] }`.
- **FR-006** `GET /sensors/{id}/history` is documented in `app/openapi.yaml`, with the response schema and the 400 and 404 errors.

## Success criteria
- **SC-001** A client reading one day (00:00 to 00:00 next day) of an hourly sensor gets exactly 25 readings in ascending time order.

## Clarifications
- 2026-10-20: The maximum range is 7 days, decided by the lead. `to - from > 7 days` is 400 `range_too_large`; exactly 7 days is allowed.
- 2026-10-20: The lead asked for `GET /sensors/{id}/history` to be added to `app/openapi.yaml` as part of this spec (FR-006).
- Response shape (`sensorId` plus `readings` array) follows the `latest` endpoint's `sensorId`/`at`/`value` fields.
- The 400 error code `range_too_large` and the inclusive bounds are decided here, following the `Error` machine-code convention in `app/openapi.yaml`.
- Out of scope: missing or malformed `from`/`to`, `from` after `to`, error precedence (e.g. unknown sensor combined with an oversized range), and timestamp formats other than ISO-8601 UTC as used in the cases.
